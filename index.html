privacy, in plain words

Your names, handled carefully.

When you press “let the magic begin”, the two submitted names and calculated result are stored for FLAMES testing, along with a random session ID and the date. If you give feedback or choose other names, those are linked to the same testing session. Names expire after 30 days; other testing records after 90 days.

Your names do not appear in a public history. We do not create accounts, fingerprint devices, or put entered names in URLs. Please do not enter someone's personal information without their permission. You can request deletion of records linked to this browser session.
